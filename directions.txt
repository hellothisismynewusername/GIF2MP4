MP4 → GIF Converter Quick Start

Requirements:

* Rust
* FFmpeg (C:\mpreg\ffmpeg.exe)
* MP4 video (usually inbuilt w windows)

Build:
cargo build --release

Run:
cargo run -- --input <input.mp4> --output <output.gif>

Optional:
--start <seconds>
--end <seconds>
--fps <number> (check by looking at properties of mp4)
--scale <percent>
--width <pixels>

Crop:
--left <percent>
--right <percent>
--up <percent>
--down <percent>

Rules: total horizontal < 100%, total vertical < 100%

Preview PNG generated automatically: <output>.preview.png
GIF < 10 MB

Examples:
cargo run -- --input .\gifex.mp4 --output gifex.gif  --fps 30  --start 230 --end 235 --up 10
